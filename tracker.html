  • Steps carry over when a tier is reached; only the target goes up.
  • Moving a path to dormant loses any steps past its last earned tier (VIII earned at 2,500 with 2,504 steps goes back to 2,500). A path unslotted before reaching tier I goes back to the available paths, not the dormant ones.
  • On the status sheet each path is tagged (B), (A) or (E) and colored by its grade: pink for basic, yellow for advanced, green for elite. A mutated path adds -M, as in (B-M), and gets a crosshatched bar.
  • The Completionist earns 10 steps per completed basic path, 20 per advanced and 40 per elite, plus a Talented/Gifted bonus. Rift paths give none.
  • Exceptionalism earns 5 steps per basic path, 10 per advanced and 15 per elite, plus variable amounts from achievements. Talented/Gifted bonuses don't apply to those, but a choose-a-path reward spent on Exceptionalism does get the bonus.
  • Progress speed scales with a race's lifespan. System text shows this as a racial multiplier against the human baseline; longer-lived races like elves progress more slowly.

Classes

  • Humans normally unlock a class at age 12. A class has its own dedicated slot and does not use one of the 10 active path slots. Bastian's shows "Locked" until then.
  • Class grades: Basic, Adept, Advanced, Elite, Master.
  • Classes can come with a range of high-tier rewards. For both basic and adept classes, the mean and median number of these rewards is 1.

Aether

  • Aether Core is volume, Aether Channels are throughput, and the Aether stat is potency.
  • Aether Core sizes use the same grades as skills: Minor, Lesser, Greater, Superior, Exalted.

Aether Core & Aether Channels scaling

Capacity in Base Minor Cores (BMC) and Base Weak Channels (BWC): a minor core 100 = 1, and having weak channels at 100 = 1. Both scale the same. Each core/channels grade holds 5× the one before (5×5×5×5×5). Reaching 200 upgrades a core/channels to 100 of the next type (e.g. Minor 200 → Lesser 100). Exalted is the highest core/channels type humanity can reach, so Exalted 200 (3,125 BMC or BWC) is the highest capacity anyone can have.

Attributes, affinities and skills

  • Basic and advanced attributes start at 100 and can keep rising past 200. Each advanced attribute has its own growth condition; path progress is the only one proven so far.
  • A Strong or higher affinity speeds up progress on related paths and on learning spells. Using a skill or casting a spell without a direct affinity for it, but with access through other affinities, costs extra aether.
  • Spellcasting requires the Aethersensing and Aether Manipulation advanced attributes. Without them, affinity-related paths can only advance through sensing an affinity and using skills of that affinity.
  • Affinities start at 100 and move up a grade automatically at 200. Grades: Weak, Moderate, Strong, Potent, Perfect. Affinities affect efficiency, which elements he can wield, and path development.
  • Skills run from 1 to 100. To advance, a skill needs double its current skill slots and an upgraded skill chosen by the user; otherwise it stays at 100. Skill power grades: Minor, Lesser, Greater, Superior, Exalted.

Advanced attributes

  • Talented (T2) gives bonus XP of its rating ÷ 5 (+20% at 100, +40% at 200) and Gifted (T3) gives its rating ÷ 2.5 (+40% at 100, +80% at 200). Fractions are dropped, both from the percent and from the bonus steps, so a 1.9-step bonus adds 1.
  • How an AA's tier relates to the bonus it gives isn't settled yet: Aether Talented, Physically Talented and Socially Talented are T1 AA's but give the same bonus as Talented. Until the story clarifies it, the tracker shows no percentage for any AA other than Talented and Gifted.
